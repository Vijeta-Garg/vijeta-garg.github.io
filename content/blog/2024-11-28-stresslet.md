<div class="blogger-post"><div>This was a project I worked on with my friends for the STEAM Tank project in May. We made it to the finals and won a couple of awards for our design!&nbsp;</div><div><br /></div><div><h1><img loading="lazy" src="https://lh7-rt.googleusercontent.com/slidesz/AGV_vUedx1BAX68uqTckbjNercOntJZkAaHxz0XMpWTQquxmNHPqfvC_TUoI_G3AQofDdCvoni45Yn4nEJepKio9GxlZTX78ZJ0GbnmApAVtoxFFKRz1hdSC8VrGeN04MS5Nku2cq3TQWJLqfg76Hnhrqpp-uaSzXFQP=w1038-h459?key=l3C1LQ0tX-oyhJ3HpIBhTQ" /></h1></div><div><br /></div><div><div><i>Our CADDed design^^^ the circuit would live inside the large "mother" bead in the middle.&nbsp;</i></div><div><i>I designed our circuitry prototype--from our schematic to the basic breadboard version.</i></div></div><div><br /></div><div>It's a bracelet that reacts to stress-- the first of its kind because bracelets like these either only focus on reacting OR monitoring to stress but we do both! Furthermore, we made it stylish and added many personalized response mechanisms so that it can be used effectively in every day life.&nbsp;</div><div><b>1) Schematic</b></div><div><span id="docs-internal-guid-ba6e05c2-7fff-20f9-04ad-1ba391664c33"><img loading="lazy" src="https://lh7-rt.googleusercontent.com/slidesz/AGV_vUd70hVl4sTmN7O6qsPA2c5awE-CtbrMIkER2iyEaUOOMC6rQ50Xo9DF2hzkJFAKPfGGcB4IYxGySejZfyq5RU-uNEydXIv3P_swqN1-uZeBHUnIvZIb5MqaGzjjdSRbgHxT9ssEP2PDJ_bT1l2mRXnwlSjgZdU=s2048?key=l3C1LQ0tX-oyhJ3HpIBhTQ" /></span></div><div>The circuit has a few different parts: There's a microprocessor that controls which "response" mechanism is getting voltage depending on the stress levels recorded and sent to it by the Pulse Sensor-- a premade monitoring Heart Beat monitoring part found online.
