TNJSF gave this amazing opportunity for the first time ever, and I took it!&nbsp;</div><div><br /></div><div><br /></div><div>I realized after getting there that everyone had their posters printed out, whereas mine looked not so...professional. I also loved how they hung up my poster like an art exhibition (look at picture above : ).&nbsp;</div><div><br /></div><div>Writing the report and abstract for my presentation also made me realize nuances to research: to search with specific keywords, create report around a "story", and of course, presentation matters (I'll show up with&nbsp; printed poster next year).&nbsp;</div><div><br /></div><div>I'm so excited about this start to my research journey and can't wait to dive deeper into the topic of E-textiles this year.</div></div>
